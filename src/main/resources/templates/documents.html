📄 Required Documents

Documents needed for admission • Session 2026

📌 Important Note Please bring all original documents along with 2 self-attested photocopies at the time of admission. Incomplete documents may result in delay or cancellation of admission. For any queries, contact the school office.
1

Collect Form

Download or collect admission form from school office

2

Fill Form

Fill all details carefully and attach required documents

3

Submit

Submit form with documents at the school office

4

Confirmation

Receive admission confirmation and fee receipt

📄 Mandatory Documents

🎉

Birth Certificate

Original birth certificate issued by Municipal Corporation or Gram Panchayat

Required
📷

Passport Size Photos

4 recent passport size photographs of the student (white background)

Required
🏫

Transfer Certificate (TC)

TC from previous school (for Class 1 and above). Not required for Nursery/LKG/UKG

Required
📋

Previous Mark Sheet

Last class mark sheet / progress report (for Class 2 and above)

Required
👤

Aadhar Card

Aadhar card of the student. If not available, parent's Aadhar card is acceptable

Required
🏠

Address Proof

Any valid address proof — Aadhar, Voter ID, Ration Card, or Electricity Bill

Required

📄 Additional Documents (If Applicable)

🏆

Caste Certificate

Required for SC/ST/OBC category students for fee concession

Optional
💰

Income Certificate

For students applying under economically weaker section (EWS) quota

Optional
🚑

Disability Certificate

For students with special needs — issued by a government medical authority

Optional
📄

Migration Certificate

Required if student is migrating from another state or board

Optional

✅ Document Checklist

Use this checklist before submitting your admission form to ensure all documents are in order:

  • Birth Certificate (Original + 2 Photocopies)
  • 4 Passport Size Photographs
  • Transfer Certificate from Previous School
  • Previous Class Mark Sheet / Progress Report
  • Aadhar Card of Student / Parent
  • Address Proof (Any valid document)
  • Filled Admission Form (Signed by Parent/Guardian)
  • Admission Fee Payment (Cash / Cheque / Online)
